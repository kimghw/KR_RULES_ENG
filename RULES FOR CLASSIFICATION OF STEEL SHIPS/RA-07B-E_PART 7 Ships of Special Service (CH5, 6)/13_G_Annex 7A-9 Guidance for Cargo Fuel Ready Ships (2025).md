# PART 7 Ships of Special Service (CH5, 6)

> RULES FOR CLASSIFICATION OF STEEL SHIPS / RA-07B-E / 2025 / EN / Guidance

## Annex 7A-9 Guidance for Cargo Fuel Ready Ships (2025)

### Section 1 General

#### 101. Application

- **1.** **This Annex applies to ships** which are prepared for conversion with the design or the partial installation for use of cargo as fuel during the new building phase(hereafter referred to as "Cargo fuel ready ships" in the Guidance) for the purpose of a conversion from a ship using conventional marine fuels to cargo fuel after delivery.
- **2.** This Guidance contains levels of readiness for use of cargo as fuel(hereafter referred to as "Cargo fuel ready levels" in the Guidance) and requirements applicable thereto, and the scope of preparation is defined by the agreement between the shipowner and the shipbuilder.
- **3.** The design and installation of cargo fuel systems for fuel-ready ships are to comply with the requirements specified in **Ch 5** of **Rules**, which are in force at the time of the construction contract for the new building. However, where a cargo fuel ready ship in accordance with this Guidance is converted to a cargo fuelled ship after delivery, the ship is to comply with **Ch 5** of **Rules** in force at the time of the ship conversion.
- **4.** In the absence of requirements for the use of such cargo fuels, the **Rules for the Classification of Ships Using Low-flashpoint Fuels** or valid guidance may be applied.

#### 102. Definitions

Unless specified in **Ch 5** of **Rules**, the definitions of terms follow the **Rules for the Classification of Ships Using Low-flashpoint Fuels**

#### 103. Cargo fuel ready levels

- **1.** **Cargo** fuel ready levels are defined in 2 steps as follows:
  - **(1)** The level of preparing the concept design
  - **(2)** The level of preparing the generic design
  - **(3)** The level of installing parts of the systems with the detailed design in addition to above (2)
- **2.** The class notations defined in **Sec 2** may be assigned where the ready level in **Para 1** above is in compliance with this Guidance.


### Section 2 Class Notation

#### 201. General

- **1.** The class notations specified in **202.** and **205.** may be assigned according to the cargo fuel ready level.
- **2.** The requirements for the class notations in this Section are to comply with **Sec 3**.

#### 202. (Cargo name) Ready D(A)

- **1.** (Cargo name) Ready D(A) as an additional special feature notation may be assigned to ships whose the cargo fuelled ship concept design is prepared for evaluation of the basic suitability. For example, 'cargo name' can be labeled as Ammonia Ready D(A), LNG Ready D(A), LPG Ready D(A), Methanol Ready D(A), etc.
- **2.** (Cargo name) Ready D(A) is not to be assigned to ships having (Cargo name) Ready D.

#### 203. (Cargo name) Ready D

- **1.** (Cargo name) Ready D as an additional special feature notation may be assigned to ships for which the generic design is prepared.
- **2.** (Cargo name) Ready D is not to be assign to ships having (Cargo name) Ready I.

#### 204. (Cargo name) Ready I

- **1.** (Cargo name) Ready I as an additional special feature notation may be assigned to ships for which parts of the systems are installed with the detailed design in addition to the generic design.
- **2.** In assignment of the (Cargo name) Ready I, the characters corresponding to the installed items may be assigned in the bracket one or a combination of them in addition to (Cargo name) Ready I. The characters corresponding to the installed items are as follows:
  - **(1)** Cargo fuel supply systems - FS
  - **(2)** Cargo fuelled main engines - ME
  - **(3)** Cargo fuelled auxiliary engines - AE
  - **(4)** Cargo fuelled boilers - B
  - **(5)** Main engines that can be converted to cargo fuel operation - ME-C
  - **(6)** Auxiliary engines that can be converted to cargo fuel operation - AE-C
  - **(7)** Boilers that can be converted to cargo fuel operation - B-C
    For example, (Cargo name) Ready I(FS, ME) may be assigned to the ship on which cargo fuel supply systems and cargo fuelled main engines are installed.


### Section 3 Requirements for Levels of Cargo Fuel Ready

#### 301. General

- **1.** This Guidance prescribes plans to be submitted and systems to be installed for cargo fuel ready levels. The design and installation of structures and systems are to be in accordance with applicable requirements in **Ch 5** of **Rules.**
- **2.** Drawing approval and survey for cargo fuel ready are not accepted as drawing approval and survey for conversion to cargo fuel ship. When the ship is converted, drawing approval and survey are to be carried out in accordance with **Ch 5** of **Rules** in force at the time of the ship conversion. Approved Drawings and certifications from new building stage may be used as reference for conversion.

#### 302. General Level of Preparing Concept Design

- **1.** Plans and documents required for an Approval in Principle (AIP) are to be submitted for (Cargo name) Ready D(A). The list of plans and documents to be submitted may be adjusted after consultation with the Society
- **2.** The plans and documents required in this Section is to be marked "(Cargo name) Ready" to separate them from the normal plans and documents of new building.

#### 303. General Level of Preparing Generic Design

- **1.** **General**
  - **(1)** This Section prescribes plans and documents to be submitted for (Cargo name) Ready D. The detail requirements for designs are to be in accordance with applicable requirements in Ch 5 of Rules.
  - **(2)** The plans and documents required in this Section is to be marked "(Cargo name) Ready" to separate them from the normal plans and documents of new building.
  - **(3)** Where parts of plans and documents required in this Section are not available, alternative documents may be accepted by the Society's review.
- **2.** **Plans and documents to be submitted**
  - **(1)** The following plans and documents are to be submitted to the Society for review
    - **(A)** General arrangement plans showing location of:
      - **(a)** Machinery spaces, accommodation, service and control station spaces
      - **(b)** Cargo fuel supply/preparation room
      - **(c)** Cargo fuel piping routing from cargo tanks to engines
      - **(d)** Ventilating pipes, doors and openings to cargo fuel supply/preparation room and other hazardous areas
      - **(e)** Entrances, air inlets and openings to accommodation, service and control station spaces
      - **(f)** Hazardous areas of zone 0, 1 and 2
      - **(g)** Toxic zone (if applicable)
    - **(B)** Following plans and data of cargo fuel supply systems:
      - **(a)** Arrangement of engine room, cargo fuel supply/preparation room and other spaces containing cargo fuel equipment
      - **(b)** Cargo fuel supply piping diagram
    - **(C)** Data for a risk analysis according to **Ch 3 Sec 2 of the Rules for the Classification of Ships Using Low-flashpoint Fuels**.
    - **(D)** Plans and data for equipment and systems regarding fire protection and extinguishing

#### 304. Level of Installing Parts of Systems

- **1.** **General**
  - **(1)** This Section prescribes parts of the systems to be installed and plans and documents to be submitted for (Cargo name) Ready I. The detail requirements for designs and installation of installed systems are to be in accordance with applicable requirements in Ch 5 of Rules.
  - **(2)** The plans and documents for generic design required in **Sec 2** are to be submitted and reviewed by the Society except those required for approval in **2.** to **8.**
  - **(3)** Parts of the systems are categorized in the follows:
    - **(A)** Cargo fuel supply systems
    - **(B)** Cargo fuelled main engines
    - **(C)** Cargo fuelled auxiliary engines
    - **(D)** Cargo fuelled boilers
    - **(E)** Main engines that can be converted to cargo fuel operation
    - **(F)** Auxiliary engines that can be converted to cargo fuel operation
    - **(G)** Boilers that can be converted to cargo fuel operation
  - **(4)** The parts which are installed on board are to be reflected in the normal plans of new building and "(Cargo name) Ready" is not to be marked on those plans.
- **2.** **Cargo fuel supply systems**
  - **(1)** Cargo fuel supply systems are to be installed in accordance with the regulations specificed in 101. 4 of this Guidance.
  - **(2)** The following plans and documents are to be submitted to the Society for approval.
    - **(A)** Arrangement of engine room, fuel supply/preparation room and other spaces containing cargo fuel equipment
    - **(B)** Ventilation system arrangement of engine room, fuel preparation room and other spaces containing cargo fuel equipment
    - **(C)** Drawings and specifications of cargo fuel supply piping
    - **(D)** Drawings and specifications of offsets, loops, bends and mechanical expansion joints, such as bellows, slip joints(only inside tank) or similar means in the cargo fuel piping
    - **(E)** Drawings and specifications of flanges, valves and other fittings in the cargo fuel piping system. For valves intended for piping systems with a design temperature below - 55 °C, documentation for leak test and functional test at design temperature (type test) is required
    - **(F)** Documentation of type tests for expansion components in the cargo fuel piping system.
    - **(G)** Specification of materials, welding, post-weld heat treatment and non-destructive testing of cargo fuel piping
    - **(H)** Specification of pressure tests (structural and tightness tests) of cargo fuel piping
    - **(I)** Program for functional tests of all piping systems including valves, fittings and associated equipment for handling cargo fuel(liquid or vapour)
    - **(J)** Drawings and specifications of insulation for low temperature piping where such insulation is installed
    - **(K)** Specification of electrical bonding of piping
    - **(L)** Cooling or heating water system in connection with cargo fuel system, if fitted.
- **3.** **Cargo fuelled main enigine**
  Main engines are to be installed in accordance with **Sec 16** of **Pt 7 Ch 5**.
- **4.** **Cargo fuelld auxiliary engines**
  Auxiliary engines are to be installed in accordance with **Sec 16** of **Pt 7 Ch 5.**
- **5.** **Cargo fulled boilers**
  Cargo fulled boilers are to be installed in accordance with **Sec 16** of **Pt 7 Ch 5.**
- **6.** **Main engines that can be converted to gas fuel operation**
  - **(1)** Convertible-type main engines are to be installed.
  - **(2)** Following plans are to be submitted for reference:
    - **(A)** details of the conversion for cargo fuel operation
    - **(B)** list of the components that need to be replaced
    - **(C)** list of new components
- **7.** **Auxiliary engines that can be converted to gas fuel operation**
  - **(1)** Convertible-type auxiliary engines are to be installed.
  - **(2)** Following plans are to be submitted for reference:
    - **(A)** details of the conversion for cargo fuel operation
    - **(B)** list of the components that need to be replaced
    - **(C)** list of new components
- **8.** **Boilers that can be converted to gas fuel operation**
  - **(1)** Convertible-type boilers are to be installed.
  - **(2)** Following plans are to be submitted for reference:
    - **(A)** details of the conversion for cargo fuel operation
    - **(B)** list of the components that need to be replaced
    - **(C)** list of new components

#### 305. Survey

- **1.** **Classification survey during construction**
  Systems are to be tested at the shops of manufacturer and after installation on board in accordance with **Ch 5** of **Rules**.
- **2.** **Periodical surveys**
  In application of this Guidance, the general condition of the relevant systems installed on board is to be examined visually at periodical surveys for the vessels having (Cargo name) Ready I notation. The systems are to be surveyed and evaluated for the condition at time of conversion, and the scope of test will be defined depending on time elapsed from new building and maintenance level of the systems.![](images/image90.png)
