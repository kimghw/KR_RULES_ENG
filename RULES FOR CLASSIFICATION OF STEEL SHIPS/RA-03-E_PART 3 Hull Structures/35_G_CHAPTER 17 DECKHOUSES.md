# PART 3 Hull Structures

> RULES FOR CLASSIFICATION OF STEEL SHIPS / RA-03-E / 2025 / EN / Guidance

## CHAPTER 17 DECKHOUSES

### Section 1 General

#### 101. Application 【See Rule】

In application to **101. 2** of the Rules, the construction and scantlings of the superstructures and deckhouses above the third tier are to be applied as if they are in third tier. ![](images/image99.png)
