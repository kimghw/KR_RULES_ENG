## CHAPTER 7 STABILITY (2017)

### Section 1 General Requirements of Stability

#### 104. Assumption of the damage extent

- **1.** **Damage extent of self-elevating units**
  Notwithstanding the requirements of **Ch 7, 104. 1 (7)** of the Rules, for self-elevating units without propulsion machinery, bottom damage may be omitted. ![](images/image5.png)
