## CHAPTER 10 ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT AND CONTROL

### Section 1 Electrical Equipment

#### 101. General

The electrical equipment is to comply with relevant requirements in **Pt 6, Ch 1** of **Rules for the Classification of Steel Ships** and **Pt 7, Ch 5 Sec 10** of **Rules for the Classification of Steel Ships.** However, **Ch 10, 108. 1 Guidance for Floating Production Units**, restricted service units may comply with the followings.


### Section 2 Control Systems

#### 201. General

The control system is to comply with relevant requirements in **Pt 7, Ch 5 Sec 13** of **Rules for the Classification of Steel Ships.** However, the control system for regasification plant is to comply with relevant requirements in **Ch 12.** ![](images/image3.png)
