# Guidance for Ships Carrying CNG In Bulk

> OTHER RULES AND GUIDANCE / GC-05-E / 2025 / EN / Guidance

## CHAPTER 1 GENERAL

### Section 1 General

#### 101. Application

- **1.** This Guidance is to apply to ships(hereinafter referred to as "ships carrying CNG in bulk") regardless of their size, including those of less than 500 tons gross tonnage, engaged in carriage of compressed natural gas in bulk.
- **2.** This Guidance is to apply to cargo containments, cargo handling systems and related system. And the requirements not specified in this Guidance such as hull, machinery and equipment, etc. are to be in accordance with the Rules for the Classification of Steel Ships(hereinafter referred to as "the Rules").
- **3.** The Structures and equipments of the ships carrying CNG in bulk is to be accepted by the flag state and the respective port authorities.

#### 102. Equivalence

Special equipment, which is not appropriate to apply the requirements of this Guidance or not specified in this Guidance, may be accepted by the Society provided that the Society is satisfied that such equipment is equivalent to or above those complying with the requirements of this Guidance.

#### 103. Classification

The Classification is to be in accordance with the requirements specified in **Pt 1** of the Rules.

#### 104. Class notations

The class notations assigned to the ships carrying CNG in bulk classed with the Society are to be in accordance with the requirements specified in **Pt 1, Ch 1, 201.** of the Rules. However, the notation "Compressed Natural Gas Carrier" is to be assigned as a ship type notation. ![](images/image5.png)
