# Guidance for Ships Carrying CNG In Bulk

> OTHER RULES AND GUIDANCE / GC-05-E / 2025 / EN / Guidance

## CHAPTER 2 CLASSIFICATION SURVEYS

### Section 1 General

#### 101. General

The classification surveys of ships carrying CNG in bulk, except where specially required in this Guidance, are to comply with the requirements specified in **Pt 1** of the Rules.


### Section 2 Periodical Surveys

#### 201. Annal Survey

Additional requirements to ship types for Annual Survey is to be in accordance with the relevant requirements specified in **Pt 1, Ch 2, 204. 3** of the Rules.

#### 202. Intermediate Survey

Additional requirements to ship types for Intermediate Survey is to be in accordance with the relevant requirements specified in **Pt 1, Ch 2, 304. 3** of the Rules.

#### 203. Special Survey

Additional requirements to ship types for Special Survey is to be in accordance with the relevant requirements specified in **Pt 1, Ch 2, Sec 5-2, Par 3** of the Rules. However, where alternative means of inspection for inside of cargo tank is provided in accordance with **305. 3.** (1) (A) of this Guidance, the inspection for inside of cargo tank is to be in accordance with the approved method. ![](images/image6.png)
