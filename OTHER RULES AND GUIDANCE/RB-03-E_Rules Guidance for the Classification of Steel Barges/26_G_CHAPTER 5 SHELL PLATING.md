## CHAPTER 5 SHELL PLATING

### Section 4 Shell Plating for End Parts

#### 401. Shell plating for end parts 【See Rules】

- **1.** The thickness of shell plating in the curved portions for 0.3$L$ from each end of barge may be calculated using the spacing, $S$, equal to the vertical or horizontal spacing of frames multiplied by 1.1. (See **Fig 5.1**)
- **2.** The tapering of the thickness of shell plating is to be as per shown in **Fig 5.2.** The thickness of each plating is not to be less than the shown in **Fig 5.2** approximately at the longitudinal center of the plating.
