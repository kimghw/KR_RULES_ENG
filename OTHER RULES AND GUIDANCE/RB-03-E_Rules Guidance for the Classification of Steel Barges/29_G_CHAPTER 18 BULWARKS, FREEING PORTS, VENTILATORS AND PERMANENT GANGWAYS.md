# Rules/Guidance for the Classification of Steel Barges

> OTHER RULES AND GUIDANCE / RB-03-E / 2025 / EN / Guidance

## CHAPTER 18 BULWARKS, FREEING PORTS, VENTILATORS AND PERMANENT GANGWAYS

### Section 2 Bulwarks and Guardrails

#### 201. General 【See Rules】

For unmanned barges, guardrails or bulwarks may be omitted.


### Section 3 Freeing Ports (2019)

#### 301. Freeing arrangements 【See Rule】

- **1.** **General**
  Where coamings or other structures are to be provided for retaining deck cargo in pontoon barges, adequate freeing ports are to be provided in accordance with **Par 2**.
- **2.** **Freeing port area**
  - **(1)** Basic area
    The basic freeing port area is to be in accordance with **Pt 4, Ch 4** of the **Rules**, but a sheer correction need not be applied.
  - **(2)** Beam Correction
    The freeing port area as determined by (1), above, may then be reduced by the factor $f _{b}$ expressed by:
    $f _{b} = (b /B) ^{2} /0.85$
    $b$ = transverse distance between the coamings (m)
    $B$ = breadth of the barge (m)
  - **(3)** Height Correction
    Where the height, $h _{b}$, of the coaming sides is greater than standard height $h _{s}$, the freeing port area as determined by (1), above, may be reduced by the factor $f _{h}$, given in table below. ![](images/image15.png)

    | $h _{b} /h _{s}$ | $f _{h}$ |
    | --- | --- |
    | 1.0 | 1.00 |
    | 2.0 | 0.66 |
    | 3.0 | 0.44 |
    | Values of $f _{h}$ for intermediate values of $h _{b} /h _{s}$ are to be obtained by linear interpolation. |   |

    $h _{b}$ = height of the coaming sides (m)
    $h _{s}$ = $0.01 L + 1.05$ (m, $75 \leq L \leq 125$)
    $L$ = length of the barge (m)
