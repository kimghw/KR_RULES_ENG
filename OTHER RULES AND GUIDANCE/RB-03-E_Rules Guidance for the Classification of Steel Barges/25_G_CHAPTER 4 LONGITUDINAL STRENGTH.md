# Rules/Guidance for the Classification of Steel Barges

> OTHER RULES AND GUIDANCE / RB-03-E / 2025 / EN / Guidance

## CHAPTER 4 LONGITUDINAL STRENGTH

### Section 1 General

#### 101. Loading manual 【See Rules】

- **1.** For chemical barges, gas barges with $L _{f}$ less than 65 m, other barges with $L _{f}$ less than 100 m and the ships engaged in under the coastal service, loading manual are not required. But precautions for loading such as design max. cargo load are to be recorded in adequate document like as stability material.
- **2.** The loading manual to be approved by the Society according to 101. of the rules, are to be prepared in accordance with **Pt 3, Annex 3-1** "Guidance for Survey and Composition of Loading Manual" of "Guidance Relating to the Rules for the Classification of Steel Ships". They are to written with a language easily understood by the shipmaster and if it is not English, English version is to be attached. ![](images/image5.png)
