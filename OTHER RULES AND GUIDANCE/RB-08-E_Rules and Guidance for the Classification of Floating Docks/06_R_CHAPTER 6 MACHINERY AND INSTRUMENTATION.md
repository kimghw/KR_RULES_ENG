# Rules and Guidance for the Classification of Floating Docks

> OTHER RULES AND GUIDANCE / RB-08-E / 2025 / EN / Rules

## CHAPTER 6 MACHINERY AND INSTRUMENTATION

### Section 1 Machinery

#### 101. Machinery

The pressure vessels other than those belonging to Group 3 and essential machinery such as generator driving and auxiliary machinery which are necessary for operations of the docks, are generally to be in accordance with the relevant provisions of the **Rules for the Classification of Steel Ships**.

#### 102. Piping system

- **1.** The piping systems are generally to be in accordance with **Pt. 6 of the Rules for the Classification of Steel Ships** as far as applicable.
- **2.** The dock is to have at least two water ballast pumps driven by each independent power. The arrangements for de-ballasting are to be such that in case of failure to the one pump an alternative pumping is available for each ballast tank.

#### 103. Electrical equipment

- **1.** Electrical equipment to be installed in the manner of minimizing the risks due to electrical cause such as electric shocks, fires etc. on reference to the requirements of the **Rules for the Classification of Steel Ships**.
- **2.** Electrical machinery and cables are to be those conformed to an applicable standards accepted by the Society, and suitable to be used safely and effectively under the conditions of the environment where they are installed.
- **3.** Electric circuits are to be protected against accidental over currents including short circuit. These protecting devices are to be capable of breaking a fault circuit, eliminating the expansion of the faults and the hazards of fire and securing to serve electric power to essential driving sources, lights, internal communications and alarm devices.
- **4.** **Supply voltage** *(2018)*
  In general, the voltage for the electric power supply to the dock is not to exceed 15 kV. Higher voltages will be subject to special consideration.
- **5.** **Switchboard** *(2018)*
  - **(1)** Switchboard for shore side power supply is to be of the metal enclosed and dead-front type. The following instruments are to be provided for each voltage level.
    · Voltmeter
    · Ammeter
    · Ground fault indication
  - **(2)** Switchboard is to be braced for maximum available fault current, and circuit breakers and fuses are to be capable of interrupting maximum fault current at their point of application. Fault current studies are to be submitted for approval and are to include determination of available feeder source current at the service disconnect switch.
