## ANNEX 3 Bending Strength Test of Bulkhead Laminates of Structural Plywood

### 1. Bending Strength Test of Bulkhead Laminates of Structural Plywood

#### (1) Specimen

As shown in **Fig 3.1**, 2 pieces of rectangular test specimens of which dimension is 50 $\mathrm{mm}$ at the right angle to fibrous direction of the plywood's surface and $20t+50 \mathrm{mm}$ at the parallel to fibrous direction, and 50 $\mathrm{mm}$ at the parallel to fibrous direction and $20t+500 \mathrm{mm}$ at the right angle to fibrous direction, are to be taken from each test sample, respectively.
![Fig 3.1 Bending Strength Specimens of Structural Plywood](https://kr-rule.krs.co.kr/Files/Document/GB-07-E/2020/image29.png)
Fig 3.1 Bending Strength Specimens of Structural Plywood

#### (2) Test Procedures

Test is to be carried out under the parallel condition between the span direction and fibrous direction of the plywood's surface as shown in **Fig 3.2**. Upper and lower load of linear region are to be measured and then calculate the modulus of bending elasticity and bending strength by the following formula.
![Fig 3.2 Bending Strength Specimens of Structural Plywood](https://kr-rule.krs.co.kr/Files/Document/GB-07-E/2020/image30.png)
Fig 3.2 Bending Strength Specimens of Structural Plywood
Modulus of bending elasticity = $\frac{pl ^{3}}{4bh ^{3} y} \mathrm{N/cm ^{2} }$
Bending strength = $\frac{3P _{b} l}{2bh ^{2}} \mathrm{N/cm ^{2} }$
$P _{b}$ : Maximum load($\mathrm{N}$)
$l$ : Length of span($\mathrm{cm}$)
$b$ : Width of specimen($\mathrm{cm}$)
$h$ : Thickness of specimen($\mathrm{cm}$)
$p$ : Difference between upper load and lower load of linear region($\mathrm{N}$)
$y$ : Deflection at the center of span corresponding to $p$. ![](images/image31.png)
