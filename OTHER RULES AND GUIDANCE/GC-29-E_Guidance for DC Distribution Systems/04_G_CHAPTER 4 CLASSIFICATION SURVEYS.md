# Guidance for DC Distribution Systems

> OTHER RULES AND GUIDANCE / GC-29-E / 2025 / EN / Guidance

## CHAPTER 4 CLASSIFICATION SURVEYS

### Section 1 General

#### 101. General

- **1.** All electrical equipment applied to DC distribution systems shall be tested and inspected in accordance with the relevant requirements of the **Rules for the Classification of Steel Ships**.
- **2.** The short-circuit protection of the system shall be verified by testing.
- **3.** It shall be demonstrated by tests that it operates without abnormality under the voltage variations shown in the **Table 2.1**.


### Section 2 Testing and Inspection

#### 201. Shop tests

- **1.** The relevant equipment of the DC distribution system shall be tested by the manufacturer.
- **2.** Procedures for testing and inspection shall include at least visual inspection, functional test and software test.
- **3.** The nominal voltage shall be applied for the testing.
- **4.** The load requirements can differ from the actual on-board requirements and if not practical run on-board after installation.
- **5.** Relevant tests shall be performed in addition to tests for the electric propulsion and power systems as defined by IEC 60092 (all parts).
- **6.** Voltage variability tests for power systems and components shall demonstrate that they meet the specified voltage variation requirements. (See **Table 2.1**)

#### 202. On-board tests

- **1.** Satisfactory operation and load tests at the manufacturer's factory, on-board and sea trials shall be verified by the Society.
- **2.** After installation, all systems shall be tested according to an approved test plan.
- **3.** In addition to basic test items, ships with DC distribution shall be subjected to at least the following tests if applicable.
  - **(1)** Verification of active load sharing between power sources
  - **(2)** Verification of reactive load sharing between power sources
  - **(3)** Test of start and stop of engines
  - **(4)** Test of system stability during fault
  - **(5)** Verification of ride through under fault conditions
  - **(6)** Testing of pre-charge system in inverters when re-connected
  - **(7)** Sudden load disconnection and load ramp-up to verify battery system capability
  - **(8)** Test of dynamic available power with batteries as power source
  - **(9)** Test of maximum charging capabilities of batteries
  - **(10)** Verification of all battery charging arrangements (on-board and on-shore when relevant)
  - **(11)** Test of alarm and safety systems
  - **(12)** Emergency shutdown test
- **4.** A test may be performed as part of the trials to verify that the quality of the power supply meets the quality set by the system designer.


### Section 3 Testing and Inspections of DC Circuit-breakers

#### 301. Low-voltage DC circuit-breakers (DC 1.5 kV or less)

- **1.** Low-voltage DC circuit-breakers shall be type approved and/or tested in accordance with relevant requirements of this Chapter. However, the ambient temperature may be modified if necessary.
- **2.** Products not specified for marine use shall meet the environmental test requirements for marine use (**Ch 3**, **Sec 23** of **Guidance for Approval of Manufacturing Process and Type Approval, etc.**).
