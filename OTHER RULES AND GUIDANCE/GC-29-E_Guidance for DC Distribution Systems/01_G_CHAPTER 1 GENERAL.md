# Guidance for DC Distribution Systems

> OTHER RULES AND GUIDANCE / GC-29-E / 2025 / EN / Guidance

## CHAPTER 1 GENERAL

### Section 1 General

#### 101. Application

- **1.** This Guidance apply to the ships intended to be registered as ships provided with DC distribution systems.
- **2.** This Guidance apply to ship’s electrical equipment and control systems supplying power for essential services and services for habitability through DC distribution systems and include requirements for design, installation and operation of DC distribution systems and related electrical equipment.
- **3.** Items not specified in this Guidance are to be in accordance with each relevant requirement of **Rules for the Classification of Steel Ships** except for the requirements inapplicable to DC distribution systems.
- **4.** Items not included in this Guidance may comply with ISO, IEC, KS or equivalent recognized standards by the appropriate consideration of the Society.

#### 102. Definitions

The definitions of terms are to follow **Rules for the Classification of Steel Ships**, unless otherwise specified in this Guidance.

- **1.** **"DC bus"** means a DC distribution system that interconnects DC loads and power source.
- **2.** **"DC assembly"** means a cabinet with modules interconnected with DC bus for load and power source.
- **3.** **"Essential services"** means those services essential for propulsion and steering, and safety of the ship, which are made up of "primary essential services" and "secondary essential services". Definitions and examples of such services are given in **Pt 6, Ch 1, 101. 4** (13) of the **Rules for the Classification of Steel Ships**.
- **4.** **"Services for habitability"** means those services which need to be in operation for maintaining the vessel's minimum comfort conditions for the crew and passengers. Examples of equipment for maintaining conditions of habitability are given in **Pt 6, Ch 1, 101. 4** (14) of the **Rules for the Classification of Steel Ships**.
- **5.** **"Energy management system"** means the upper management system of the battery system including a monitoring and control system for energy capacity. When the function of the energy management system is included in the power management system, the power management system is regarded as an energy management system.
- **6.** **"Battery management system"** means a device for monitoring the charge/discharge status to that the battery can be efficiently managed by measuring the values of current, voltage, temperature, etc. and for safely controlling the function of the battery such as operating the safety device in case of abnormal operation.
- **7.** **"Battery system"** means an independently operable device connected to the battery control device and an assembly in which one or more modules or battery packs are connected in series or in parallel. And battery system may include a power converter for charging/discharging the battery.
- **8.** **"Electric power converter"** a device capable of both of the function of suppling power to the power system by converting the designated DC power from the battery to AC or suppling power directly to the AC load and the function to charge the battery by converting AC power from the power system to DC.
- **9.** **"Inrush current"** means a transient current that occurs instantaneously when power is supplied to the electrical equipment.
- **10.** **"Common-mode noise"** means an interference signal generated on the same phase.
- **11.** **"High frequency noise"** means all signals whose frequency is greater than 1000 times the fundamental frequency and whose voltage is less than or equal to twice the RMS value of the power system, and includes a small level of impulse.
- **12.** **"IT system"** means earthing system that isolates all energized parts from the earth or grounds one point of the power supply to ground through an impedance to connect the exposed conductive parts of the electrical equipment either individually or collectively or to the ground of the system.
- **13.** **"Insulation monitoring device (IMD)"** means a device that monitors the insulation resistance of a IT system.
- **14.** **"Insulation fault locator system (IFLS)"** means a system that finds an insulation fault location in an IT system.
- **15.** **"Arc flash"** means a sort of air discharge phenomenon that can break down the insulation between the charging conductors and cause a fire or a burn.
- **16.** **"Personal protective equipment (PPE)"** means any device or apparatus that is worn to protect persons from danger.

#### 103. Equivalency

The equivalence of alternative and novel features which deviate from or are not directly applicable to the Guidance is to be in accordance with **Pt 1**, **Ch 1**, **104**. of **Rules for the Classification of Steel Ships**. *(2020)*

#### 104. Modification of requirements

Since DC distribution technology will be under constant development, the requirements of this Guidance may need to be supported by additional information and requirements, on a case by case basis. Designs that are not in compliance with this Guidance may be approved after evaluation by the Society, provided that it can be demonstrated that the design represents an equal or better level of safety.
