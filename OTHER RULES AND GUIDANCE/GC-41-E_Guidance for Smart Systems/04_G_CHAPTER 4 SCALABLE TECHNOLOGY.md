# Guidance for Smart Systems

> OTHER RULES AND GUIDANCE / GC-41-E / 2025 / EN / Guidance

## CHAPTER 4 SCALABLE TECHNOLOGY

### Section 1 Virtual Reality (VR)

#### 101. General

- **1.** Virtual reality(VR) means a virtual expression in which information of equipment installed on the ship is input as data for the purpose of use in the ship’s operation and information of the ship can be checked through VR devices.
- **2.** Technology using VR devices can be used for purpose of crew training and ship operation, and VR-related facilities can be installed and implemented in a designated center on-board or on land.
- **3.** Ship-related information used in VR can be used as basic data to implement a digital twin.

#### 102. VR Configuration

- **1.** How to accumulate information necessary for VR implementation.
  - **(1)** 3D modeling
  - **(2)** 360 degree shooting with camera
- **2.** Equipment required for VR implementation.
  - **(1)** Hardware and software used to create/store data
  - **(2)** VR headset
  - **(3)** Monitor for displaying VR information


### Section 2 Augmented Reality (AR)

#### 201. General

- **1.** Augmented Reality(AR) means a technology that collects and analyzes information on the internal and external conditions of a ship, and can check the ship's route and surrounding information(ships, marine objects, etc.) through facilities installed on the ship or on land.
- **2.** Technology using AR devices can be directly used for navigation and crew training, and AR-related facilities can be installed and implemented in a designated center on-board or on land.
- **3.** Ship-related information used in AR can be used as basic data to implement a digital twin.

#### 202. AR Configuration

- **1.** How to accumulate information necessary for AR implementation.
  - **(1)** Collecting internal and external information of the ship through the sensor installed on the ship.
  - **(2)** Collecting ship and marine environment information through external communication.
- **2.** Equipment required for AR implementation.
  - **(1)** Hardware and software used to create/store data
  - **(2)** Monitor for displaying VR information. ![](images/image3.png)
