# Guidance for Smart Systems

> OTHER RULES AND GUIDANCE / GC-41-E / 2025 / EN / Guidance

## CHAPTER 2 CLASSIFICATION SURVEYS

### Section 1 Classification Surveys

#### 101. Classification Survey during Construction

- **1.** **General**
  - **(1)** The smart system installation and commissioning survey is to be carried out on-board the vessel.
  - **(2)** During the installation and commissioning survey, the Surveyor shall be present and verify the Smart Function.
  - **(3)** Upon completion of the installation and commissioning survey, the vessel and its associated systems will be eligible for assignment of the applicable optional notations.
- **2.** Initial survey
  - **(1)** Proper installation of the equipment related smart infrastructure
  - **(2)** Proper provision on board of all relevant documents, procedures manuals and record books
- **3.** **Commissioning**
  - **(1)** Integration is to be tested to verify that intended capability has been achieved.
  - **(2)** Verify each smart system function, such as recording and display of acquired data (if applicable).
  - **(3)** Check data acquisition/storage/processing

#### 102. Periodical Survey for Maintaining Registration

- **1.** **Annual Survey**
  - **(1)** Verify the availability of smart system function operations Manual.
  - **(2)** Confirm that any modifications or changes to the hardware and software of the smart system are documented and kept on-board.
  - **(3)** Confirm that the hardware and software of the smart system are operational. ![](images/image3.png)
