# Guidance for Ships designed to Prevent the spread of Infectious Disease

> OTHER RULES AND GUIDANCE / GC-40-E / 2025 / EN / Guidance

## Section 1 General

### 101. Application

#### 1. This guidance provides design and survey requirements to prevent the spread of infectious disease through isolation of suspected and confirmed persons, and separation of shore personnel and visitors from existing crew in the event of an outbreak of an infectious disease on board (hereinafter referred to as “outbreak”).

#### 2. The requirements of this guidance are optional, and ships satisfying the requirements of this guidance may be assigned a notation specified in 103. as additional special feature notations.


### 102. Definitions

The definitions of terms in this guidance are as follows.

#### 1. Negative pressure isolation cabin is to be a cabin with a sanitary space maintaining continuous negative pressure to isolate suspected and confirmed persons in the event of an outbreak. The sanitary space can be accessed directly from the cabin, and a toilet and a shower facility are to be installed.

#### 2. Anteroom is to be a small space located between the negative pressure isolation cabin and the corridor as a space to prevent air infection as well as a preparation space for carrying out infection prevention management.

#### 3. HEPA filter or equivalent filter is to be a HEPA filter (removing more than 99.97% of 0.3 particles) or a filter with equivalent decontamination capability.


### 105. Drawings and documents to be submitted

#### 1. Where ships are intended to be assigned the notation of PID, the drawings and documents listed below are to be submitted to the Society for approval prior to commencement of construction. In addition, if deemed necessary by the Society, additional drawings and documents other than those specified below may be requested.

- **(1)** Accommodation plans for each deck related to the design to prevent the spread of infectious disease
- **(2)** Schematics drawings, layout drawings and design specifications for ventilation system related to the design to prevent the spread of infectious disease
- **(3)** List of finishing materials such as ceilings, floors, walls of negative pressure isolation cabins, anterooms, hospital rooms, sanitary spaces and offices designated for shore personnel and visitors and storage space of infectious solid waste
